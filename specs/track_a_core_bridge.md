# Track A: Core + Bridge

## Definition
Track A includes roles that either:
- directly support a long-term technical career path (Core), or
- act as credible, compounding bridge roles toward that path (Bridge)

A role must:
- align with technical, analytical, data-quality, systems, or process-focused work
- match the candidate's current skill level closely enough to allow realistic contribution
- not introduce reputational or environmental risk

If a role fails Track A, it should be evaluated under Track B (Buffer).

---

# Scoring System Overview

Total score: 100 points
15 categories with fixed weights

---

## Category 1 - Core career-path alignment (Weight: 12)

Does the role move the candidate toward:
- backend / systems / platform
- QA / data quality engineering
- structured internal technical work

Green:
- directly aligned with target or strong adjacent lane

Amber:
- useful bridge, but mixed alignment

Red:
- pushes toward analyst, stakeholder-heavy, or socially driven work

---

## Category 2 - Technical systems fit (Weight: 8)

Does the daily work involve:
- systems
- structured logic
- validation / correctness
- technical problem-solving

Green:
- real systems work, correctness-driven

Amber:
- some systems work, diluted

Red:
- mostly coordination, reporting, or business translation

---

## Category 3 - Barrier to entry (Weight: 6)

Does the role's entry threshold match the candidate's current level?

Green:
- clearly junior, strong match

Amber:
- stretch but plausible

Red:
- expects skills the candidate does not yet have commercially

---

## Category 4 - Day-1 usefulness (Weight: 6)

Can the candidate provide useful value early?

Green:
- immediate contribution via validation, QA, structured work

Amber:
- partial early contribution

Red:
- usefulness depends on skills the candidate does not yet have

---

## Category 5 - Stakeholder load (Weight: 8)

How much success depends on:
- coordination
- persuasion
- relationship management

Green:
- low stakeholder dependence

Amber:
- moderate internal coordination

Red:
- high stakeholder / relationship-driven role

---

## Category 6 - Ambiguity level (Weight: 8)

How clear are expectations and success criteria?

Green:
- clear tasks, clear standards

Amber:
- some uncertainty

Red:
- vague role, unclear expectations

---

## Category 7 - Structure / predictability (Weight: 6)

How stable is the environment?

Green:
- organised, predictable workflow

Amber:
- some inconsistency

Red:
- chaotic, reactive environment

---

## Category 8 - Ramp-up realism (Weight: 6)

Can the candidate realistically stabilise in this role?

Green:
- achievable ramp-up

Amber:
- stretch but possible

Red:
- expectations too fast or too steep

---

## Category 9 - Internal vs client-facing (Weight: 7)

How exposed is the role to external pressure?

Green:
- internal-facing

Amber:
- some external interaction

Red:
- client-facing / externally driven

---

## Category 10 - Pressure / operational load (Weight: 7)

Is the role built around urgency and escalation?

Green:
- controlled pace

Amber:
- some pressure

Red:
- constant urgency / escalation

---

## Category 11 - Work mode / location stability (Weight: 4)

Is the work setup stable and predictable?

Green:
- clear, consistent setup

Amber:
- some instability

Red:
- unpredictable or disruptive logistics

---

## Category 12 - Training / support (Weight: 5)

Is there enough support for the candidate's current level?

Green:
- clear onboarding, feedback, guidance

Amber:
- some support, unclear

Red:
- minimal support, sink-or-swim

Rule:
Low support is acceptable only if role has low stretch.

---

## Category 13 - Narrative value (Weight: 6)

Does this strengthen the candidate's career story?

Green:
- reinforces a technical individual-contributor trajectory

Amber:
- partial signal

Red:
- creates narrative drift

---

## Category 14 - Support and working environment fit (Weight: 6)

Is the environment fair and stable?

Green:
- constructive feedback, clear expectations

Amber:
- mixed signals

Red:
- political, punitive, or unstable culture

---

## Category 15 - Stability / progression (Weight: 5)

Does this role improve the candidate's future position?

Green:
- builds leverage and progression

Amber:
- some value

Red:
- holding-pattern role

---

# Track A Decision Rule

Apply only if:

- Total score >= 70
- No critical red flags
- Role does not violate:
  - ramp-up realism
  - support fit
  - stakeholder load limits

Score bands:
- 80-100 -> Apply immediately
- 70-79 -> Apply
- <70 -> Reject from Track A

---

# Reroute Rule

If a role fails Track A:

-> Re-evaluate under Track B (Buffer)

Only keep if:
- safe
- stable
- non-damaging
