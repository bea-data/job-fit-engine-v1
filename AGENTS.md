# Project Guidance

Use the existing rule-based evaluation flow in `job_fit_engine` and keep changes consistent with the current scoring model.

## Evaluation Rules

- Prioritise employer-specific day-to-day duties, role summaries, essential requirements, and explicit onboarding or support statements over apprenticeship-provider boilerplate or generic training-course content.
- Classify stakeholder and collaboration load as high only when there is repeated, central evidence that stakeholder-facing work is core to the role, such as business partnering, workshops, direct requirements gathering from multiple non-technical teams, presentation or persuasion ownership, or ongoing relationship management.
- Do not over-weight isolated collaboration terms such as `stakeholders`, `communicate`, `collaborative`, or `influence` when they appear without clear evidence that stakeholder-facing work is a central responsibility, especially in junior technical roles.
- Classify explicit route-based entry requirements such as `experience or qualification or apprenticeship` as `Conditional` rather than `Unclear`.
- Distinguish open-entry junior roles from junior-but-filtered roles. A junior title alone does not imply graduate-only or student-only targeting, but explicit year, recency, enrolment, or eligibility filters should still affect eligibility.
